

# Q2. Retrieval of Human Insulin Protein Sequence from NCBI

## Task
Retrieve the human insulin protein sequence from the NCBI Protein database and record its accession number, sequence length, and FASTA sequence.

## Database / Tool
- NCBI Protein Database
- NCBI Entrez

## Protein Information
- **Protein:** Insulin [Homo sapiens]
- **Organism:** *Homo sapiens*
- **Accession Number:** NP_000207.2
- **Sequence Type:** Protein
- **Sequence Length:** 110 amino acids
- **NCBI Link:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/protein/NP_000207.2

## Workflow
1. Searched the NCBI Protein database for human insulin.
2. Identified the corresponding *Homo sapiens* insulin protein record.
3. Examined the protein record and recorded the accession number and sequence length.
4. Retrieved the protein sequence in FASTA format.
